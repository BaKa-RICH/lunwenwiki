# research-argumentation

This skill improves the quality of research reasoning without changing research
facts. Use it alongside `research-wiki` when a candidate, HYP, proof, EXP
report, or DEC needs tighter mechanism logic or claim discipline.

## Route By Task

| User intent | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Turn a candidate into a mechanism argument | `references/workflows-mechanism-argument.md` |
| Write or audit a proof sketch | `references/workflows-proof-writing.md` |
| Generate strongest objections | `references/workflows-reviewer-objection.md` |
| Audit claim strength and evidence labels | `references/workflows-claim-discipline.md` |

## Boundaries

- Do not modify evidence strength.
- Do not convert `UNVERIFIED` or `INFERRED` claims into supported conclusions.
- Do not write manuscript prose for submission.
- Do not polish language for style only.
- Do not create HYP, EXP brief, EXP report, or DEC files. That belongs to
  `research-wiki` and requires its safety checks.

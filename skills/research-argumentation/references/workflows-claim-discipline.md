# Claim Discipline Workflow

Use this workflow to check that the prose does not outrun evidence.

## Checks

- `UNVERIFIED` claims are not written as supported.
- `INFERRED` claims are not presented as directly extracted.
- `AMBIGUOUS` evidence is not silently resolved.
- `weakens` or partial support is not written as success.
- EXP reports separate support, weakening evidence, anomalies, and unknowns.
- DEC rationale does not imply more than the evidence supports.

## Output

```text
Overclaims:
Evidence-label issues:
Missing objections:
Safer replacement wording:
```

Use `assets/templates/claim-audit.md`.

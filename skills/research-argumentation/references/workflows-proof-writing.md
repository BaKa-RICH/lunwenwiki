# Proof Writing Workflow

Use this workflow to make a proof sketch reach at least Level 1 mechanism
reasoning.

## Level 1 Means

Level 1 does not require formal theorem proof. It must provide:

- assumptions
- mechanism chain
- expected observable effects
- failure conditions
- what evidence would weaken the claim

## Steps

1. Restate the HYP claim.
2. List assumptions and which are extracted, inferred, ambiguous, or unverified.
3. Build a short causal/mechanism chain.
4. Map each step to expected experimental observations.
5. List failure conditions and counterexamples.
6. Conclude whether the proof is Level 0, Level 1, or blocked.

# Reviewer Objection Workflow

Use this workflow to stress-test candidates, HYP cards, proof sketches, EXP
reports, and DEC records.

## Objection Types

- module-stitching concern
- novelty concern
- closest-prior-work concern
- mechanism validity concern
- simpler-baseline concern
- experiment identifiability concern
- overclaim concern

## Output

```text
Strongest objection:
Why it matters:
Evidence needed to answer it:
Minimum revision:
```

Use `assets/templates/strongest-objection.md`.

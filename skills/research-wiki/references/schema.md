# Schema Reference

The canonical schema documents live under `schema/`:

- `schema/lifecycle.schema.md`
- `schema/page_frontmatter.schema.md`
- `schema/citation_policy.md`

Use them as documentation and lint targets, not as a replacement for
`AGENTS.md`.

Important lifecycle constraints:

- Candidates must not skip directly to EXP.
- HYP controls formal research idea state.
- Proof sketch must reach Level 1 before EXP brief.
- EXP reports record facts and HYP impact.
- DEC records final research decisions for the loop.
